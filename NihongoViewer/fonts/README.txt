The first three cover Japanese (kana + kanji) and Latin/English and are offered
in the Font dropdown. Noto Sans SC is not in the dropdown: it is used
automatically when the translate language is Simplified Chinese (the other three
lack many Simplified characters); it also covers kana for Duo mode. Noto Sans TC
is used the same way for Traditional Chinese (Taiwan glyph shapes; covers kana).
